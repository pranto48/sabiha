# Sabiha Art Gallery

A colourful, bilingual portfolio for Sabiha — a 13-year-old art explorer from Ashulia, Dhaka.
The site highlights her biography, gallery, shop, and playful 3D-inspired effects.

## Getting started

1. Open `index.html` directly in your browser or serve it via any static host (GitHub Pages, Netlify).
2. Visit `gallery.html` for the full-screen gallery wall, or use the navigation links between both pages.
3. Click the EN/BD switch in the header to browse the English or Bangla copy — your preference is saved locally.
4. Customize the gallery cards, shop items, download packs, and biography text with Sabiha's latest work.
5. Update the Unsplash image URLs with your own photos for the best experience.

## Features

- Animated hero section with glassmorphism cards and floating organic shapes.
- Dedicated gallery page featuring spotlight collections, behind-the-scenes notes, and downloadable packs.
- EN/BD toggle that translates key copy across both pages for bilingual visitors.
- Life history timeline, storytelling sections, and Netlify-ready contact form.
- Shop section for photo prints and digital downloads to support future art camps.

Enjoy celebrating Sabiha's creativity!
