Behind the Scenes

Sabiha documents each artwork like a mini science experiment so that other Bangladeshi kids can recreate the magic.

  • Collects colour palettes from local saree shops and schoolyard flowers.
  • Sketches storyboards in Bangla and English to practice bilingual storytelling.
  • Edits using free Android apps, then publishes layered files on GitHub.
