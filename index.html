About Sabiha

The Story

Growing up beside the Turag River in Ashulia, Sabiha paints vibrant skies and futuristic cities from the stories she hears at school. Her art blends Bangladeshi folk motifs with neon gradients inspired by digital games. She loves experimenting with composition, turning everyday objects into sci-fi storytelling props.

Mission

"Art is my way to protect memories." Sabiha dreams of building a community studio where teens can learn drawing, storyboarding, and 3D animation using free, open-source tools. Hosting her gallery on GitHub lets her share process files and inspire other curious builders around the world.

Skills in Progress

  • Digital sketching on Android apps
  • Stop-motion experiments with clay models
  • Three.js fundamentals for playful 3D scenes
  • Color theory and storytelling workshops

Why 3D Animation?

Sabiha is fascinated by 3D forms because they let her sculpt emotions in mid-air. She mixes hand-drawn textures with simple cubes and spheres, then animates them to float across scenes representing Dhaka's rooftops. This website uses subtle 3D-inspired hover effects to echo her creative voice.
